Meet Janet

There is power in becoming.

Glow From The Ground Up is Janet's space for sharing stories, reflections and conversations around the experiences that shape us.

It is a reminder that growth does not always happen overnight. Sometimes it happens quietly — through faith, difficult seasons, brave decisions and choosing to keep going.

Through the book, stories, conversations and media, Janet hopes to create a space where people can learn, reflect and find encouragement for their own journey.

— Janet
