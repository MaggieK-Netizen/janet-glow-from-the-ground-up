Welcome

There is more to your story.

Glow From The Ground Up is a reminder that where you start does not have to determine where you end. Through honest stories, conversations and reflections, Janet creates space for people to find courage, hope and purpose.

What We Believe

Growth can begin anywhere.

Your beginning matters.

Every experience shapes us, but no experience has to become the final definition of who we are.

Your voice matters.

There is power in sharing honest stories and creating room for others to feel seen and heard.

Your growth matters.

Becoming takes time. Every small step forward is still movement.

About Janet

A voice for becoming.

Janet believes that our stories have the power to connect, encourage and transform us.

Through writing, conversations and personal reflections, she explores the experiences that shape us — and the courage it takes to keep growing.

Glow From The Ground Up is her invitation to anyone who has ever wondered whether their beginning is too difficult, too ordinary or too painful to become something beautiful.
